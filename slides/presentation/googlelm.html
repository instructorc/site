Interactive Group Activity

This interactive Group activity will provide experience with using Gemini Notebook and generating artifacts based on uploaded material.
For this group activity, you'll simulate the process a student would take to prepare for an exam. It is assumed that the resources provided below will have already been viewed and studied by the student.

Step 1
Select someone in your group who has a Google account and who is willing to share their screen with the group.

Step 2
Navigate to https://notebook.google Upload the resources found here into your GoogleLM workspace

Step 3
Generate 3 artifacts, learn from the generated artifacts and discuss the correct option to the pre-test questions.
